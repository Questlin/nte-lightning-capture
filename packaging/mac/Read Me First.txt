NTE LIGHTNING CAPTURE FOR MAC (TEST VERSION)
=============================================

The app watches part of your screen while Neverness to Everness (NTE) is in
photo mode, and presses the game's photo key the moment lightning strikes there.

WARNING: Using this tool very likely breaks NTE's terms of use. NTE does not
allow third-party programs or automation, and players report bans for using
them. Your account could be suspended or banned. Use it at your own risk.
This is an unofficial fan project, not made or supported by NTE's developers.

You need: a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer), macOS 14 or newer, and NTE.


1. INSTALL
----------
Drag "NTE Lightning Capture.app" into your Applications folder.


2. OPEN IT THE FIRST TIME
-------------------------
Double-click the app. macOS says it can't check the app for malicious
software, because it isn't signed with an Apple developer account.

  - Click "Done".
  - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
  - Scroll down to the message about "NTE Lightning Capture" and click
    "Open Anyway". Confirm with your password or Touch ID.

You only need to do this once per version.


3. ALLOW THE THREE PERMISSIONS
------------------------------
The app asks for these when it starts. In System Settings > Privacy &
Security, turn on "NTE Lightning Capture" in each of these lists:

  - Screen & System Audio Recording  (so it can see the game)
  - Accessibility                    (so it can press the photo key)
  - Input Monitoring                 (so the arm hotkey works in the game)

Then quit the app (Command+Q) and open it again. If one is still missing,
the app's window says which.


4. CHOOSE WHERE TO LOOK FOR LIGHTNING
-------------------------------------
  - Start NTE, open photo mode (F9, active mode) and set the weather to rain.
  - Switch to NTE Lightning Capture and click "Select regions on screen...".
    The app hides and shows a screenshot of the game.
  - Drag a rectangle over the sky where lightning appears. Keep it away from
    your character, the screen edges and the on-screen controls.
  - Press Return to save. (Esc cancels. Right-click or Control-click a
    rectangle to delete it.)


5. TAKE PHOTOS
--------------
  - Switch back to the game.
  - Press fn+F6. A short sound means the app is armed.
  - When lightning strikes inside your rectangle, the app presses F to take
    the photo. By default it takes one photo and then stops.
  - Press fn+F6 again to disarm.

If NTE's photo key isn't F, change "Game's photo key/button" in the app
first. To take more than one photo, tick "Debug options" and change
"Stop after this many photos" (0 = no limit).


IF IT DOESN'T WORK
------------------
No photo is taken:
  Click "Test photo key", then switch to the game within 3 seconds. If no
  photo is taken, set "Send the key as" to "Send to game only" and test
  again. Also check that Accessibility is turned on (step 3).

fn+F6 does nothing in the game:
  Check Input Monitoring (step 3), or click "Arm" in the app instead.

The screenshot is blank or only shows the desktop:
  Check Screen & System Audio Recording (step 3), then quit and reopen.

Lightning isn't detected, or it reacts to the wrong things:
  Tick "Debug options" and click "Start watching". The bars under "Live
  signal" show what the app sees. They should stay left of the red line
  until lightning strikes. Move or shrink the rectangle if they don't.


INSTALLING A NEW VERSION
------------------------
macOS treats each new version as a different app. In each of the three
lists in step 3, select the old "NTE Lightning Capture", click the minus
button to remove it, and then allow the new one.


Settings are saved in:
  ~/Library/Application Support/NTELightningCapture/config.json
